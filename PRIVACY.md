# Privacy policy

_Gains Train (gains-train), Android app. Last updated: 30 September 2026._

**Gains Train collects no data.** Everything you enter stays on your phone, and nothing is
sent to us or anyone else.

## What the app stores, and where

The workouts, routine, exercises, rest times and settings you enter are stored only in the
app's own storage on your phone. There is no account, no server and no sync.

## What the app sends

Nothing. The app doesn't have internet access: it asks Android for no permissions at all, so
it can't send anything over the internet. There are no ads, no analytics, no crash reports
and no tracking. The exercise drawings and instructions are built into the app.

Android's cloud backup is turned off for the app, so your data isn't copied to Google's
backup either.

## Backups you make yourself

**Settings → Export backup** creates a backup file (a zip of spreadsheets) and hands it to
Android's share sheet. You choose where it goes, for example Google Drive or your phone's
Files. From then on it's your file, handled by the app or service you picked. **Import
backup** reads a backup file you choose.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes all its data
from your phone. Backup files you exported stay where you put them.

## Children

The app isn't aimed at children, and it collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date. The history of
every change is in this repository.
